What I’m building

Gombwe is my personal AI assistant. I reach it through Discord and it can actually do things on my Mac and home network, not just chat: order groceries across Coles and Woolworths, keep track of school events, plan meals, run parental controls and watch the home network. It mostly looks after itself, restarting and repairing its own feeds when something breaks.

AgentsForm is a bet I keep coming back to: that the best interface is no interface at all. An agent hits something it can’t do, describes the skill it needs, the skill gets built on demand, and it finishes the job.

AI Receptionist is a smaller build for trades and service businesses: catching the missed call, taking down the job, working out how urgent it is and handing off a booking, so a plumber or electrician stops losing work after hours.

TradeProcurement helps with tenders and RFPs: reading the document, pulling out the requirements, comparing suppliers and drafting a response, while the actual commercial call stays with a person.

I mostly work in Python, TypeScript, SQL and AWS, with a long tail of network and RF tooling behind me. The easiest way to reach me is email.

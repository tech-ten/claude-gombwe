§01Abstractsummary · what I do · why

I’m an engineer and network planning manager with fifteen years of production depth across telecommunications — RAN, fixed wireless, fibre, transmission, performance and capacity — at nbn, Ericsson, Telstra, Huawei, Astellia and Econet. The through-line has always been the same: take work that is slow, manual and repetitive and turn it into repeatable, observable systems.

Today that work has moved up the stack. I build LLM applications, tool-use and function-calling systems, API/backend services, data platforms and the AI infrastructure underneath them. The engineering discipline is identical to network automation — data models, guardrails, logs, evaluation loops — only now the systems can talk, decide and act. This page is written like a datasheet on purpose.
